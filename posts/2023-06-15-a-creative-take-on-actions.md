We held an internal design conference at GitHub this week: LGTM! (Looks Good To Me). Most of the talks will soon be available [on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL0lo9MOBetEHChcHjWv43q3BSLlicUW8s).

![LGTM conf cover](/assets/posts/lgtmcover.png)

I had fun preparing some slides about creative uses of GitHub Actions. It was great to talk about my fixation with automation, revisit the "Design tool as CMS" concept, show how to build your own "Wayback machine", and try to get the team excited about automating repetitive tasks.
