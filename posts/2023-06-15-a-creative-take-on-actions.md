I gave a talk about creative uses of GitHub Actions at LGTM! (Looks Good To Me), GitHub’s internal design conference, this week. Most of the talks will soon be available [on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL0lo9MOBetEHChcHjWv43q3BSLlicUW8s).

![LGTM conf cover](/assets/posts/lgtmcover.png)

I covered my fixation with automation, revisited the "Design tool as CMS" concept, showed how to build your own "Wayback machine", and tried to get the team excited about automating repetitive tasks.
