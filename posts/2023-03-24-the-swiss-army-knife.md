I like the idea of a Swiss Army knife more than the actual thing. Maybe I never had a good one.

Still, I used the metaphor way too many times this week. A compact, versatile tool is appealing: a collection of [small, sharp tools](https://brandur.org/small-sharp-tools) at your service.
