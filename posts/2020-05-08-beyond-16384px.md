[The Chromium version I was using can’t process images taller than 16,384 px](https://github.com/puppeteer/puppeteer/pull/937). It took me days to figure that out.

My full-page captures of long pages [were coming back full of white patches](https://fluxcapacitorprod.blob.core.windows.net/fluxcontainer/2020-05-06/desktop/enterprise.png), and I chased every other suspect first. Do images have enough time to load? Is a CSS property causing a rendering issue? What about animations?

The fix: capture, scroll, repeat, and stitch [the result](https://fluxcapacitorprod.blob.core.windows.net/fluxcontainer/2020-05-07/desktop/enterprise.png). That brings its own fun problems:

- Capturing sticky headers and footers (solved!)
- Fixed sidebar repetition (no clue how to work around this problem sustainably)
- Animation loops at the seams between captures (🤷🏼‍♂️)
