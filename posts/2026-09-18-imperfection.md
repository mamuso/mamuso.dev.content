Sometime around 2008, maybe 2009, I spent a lot of time lurking on the YayHooray forum. I vividly remember a thread about the [My Famicase Exhibition](https://famicase.com/chronicle/index.html), a yearly show put on by METEOR, a retro-game shop and gallery in Tokyo.

Designers from all over were making labels for games that didn't exist, hoping to get picked for the show.

I never submitted one. But over the years, Famicase entries kept showing up on Dribbble and Behance, usually when I wasn't looking for them. Almost twenty years later, [the exhibition is still going](https://famicase.com/).
