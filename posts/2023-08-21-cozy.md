Of all the social apps I’ve tried recently, Retro is the one I find most interesting. It has a very low barrier to posting and encourages a small following. It limits how much followers can see unless you share a key with them, and nudges you to participate instead of just lurking.

![Retro](/assets/posts/retro.png)

It’s not flashy, but it’s beautiful, and I enjoy the close-friends vibe: you get to see everyday life, kids and all. It feels like a private photo journal: focused, delightful, with no hashtags or filters.

Simply simple.
