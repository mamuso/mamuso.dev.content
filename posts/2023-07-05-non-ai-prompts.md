I have been trying to be more intentional about the photos I take, and I always go out with a few topics in mind. I’m obsessed with the beauty of decay: old buildings, abandoned places and cars, and rusty shop signs. I'm also a sucker for symmetry.

After a few unsuccessful attempts to get the kids interested in photography, I’ve been enjoying [this book](https://aperture.org/books/eyes-open-24-photography-ideas-for-curious-kids/) of photo prompts for kids. The projects are approachable, the examples are fantastic, and the layout is beautiful. The kids are already working on a photo-alphabet!
