He is talking about writing, but touches on a couple of topics that seem transferable and relevant to design:

- **Fail fast:** find out what isn’t working while there’s still room to change direction. In design, sharing early and often can help with that. I’d also note that exploring several ideas at the start pays off.
- **Giving feedback:** focus on explaining what isn’t working and why. Hold off on suggesting solutions unless people specifically ask for suggestions.
- **Receiving feedback:** synthesize it and reflect on it. When others suggest solutions, use your judgment instead of accepting them outright.
